About

The City Of Dreams

Mumbai is the capital of Maharashtra and the financial capital of India. It was once seven islands that were joined together to form one big city.

It is home to Bollywood, the biggest film industry in the world by number of films. People from all over India come here to chase their dreams, which is why it is called the City Of Dreams.
