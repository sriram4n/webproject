Shop the Streets

Where Mumbai goes shopping

01
Street Market

Colaba Causeway

Colaba, a short walk from the Gateway

South Mumbai's most famous shopping street. Its arcades are packed with stalls selling junk jewellery, antiques, bags, clothes and old books, and between finds you can refuel at classic cafés like Leopold and Mondegar.

  • Junk jewellery
  • Antiques
  • Bags
  • Clothes
  • Books
Bargain, always. Start at about half the asking price.

Photos: ghoseb (CC BY-SA 2.0), Udaykumar PR (CC BY 3.0), via Wikimedia Commons

02
Street Shopping

Bandra

Linking Road & Hill Road, Bandra West

The Queen of the Suburbs. Linking Road is lined with stalls of footwear and trendy clothes at street prices, while Hill Road is full of bags, accessories and bangles. End the day by the sea at Bandstand or Carter Road.

  • Footwear
  • Street fashion
  • Bags
  • Bangles & accessories
Go in the evening, when the stalls light up.

About

The City Of Dreams

Mumbai is the capital of Maharashtra and the financial capital of India. It was once seven islands that were joined together to form one big city.

It is home to Bollywood, the biggest film industry in the world by number of films. People from all over India come here to chase their dreams, which is why it is called the City Of Dreams.
