About

Why these cities are famous

The City Of Dreams

Mumbai

The capital of Maharashtra and the financial capital of India. Once seven separate islands, it is now one of the busiest cities in the world.

  • Home of Bollywood, India's biggest film industry
  • Bombay Stock Exchange, the oldest in Asia
  • Vada pav, pav bhaji and the local trains

The Orange City

Nagpur

Sitting almost exactly at the centre of India, Nagpur is marked by the Zero Mile Stone. It is also the winter capital of Maharashtra.

  • Famous for its sweet Nagpur oranges
  • Geographical centre of India (Zero Mile)
  • Gateway to the Tadoba and Pench tiger reserves

The City Of Gates

Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar

Formerly Aurangabad, the city once had over 50 gates. It is called the tourism capital of Maharashtra.

  • Ajanta and Ellora caves, both UNESCO World Heritage Sites
  • Bibi Ka Maqbara, the "Taj of the Deccan"
  • Hand-woven Himroo shawls and Paithani sarees

The Wine Capital Of India

Nashik

A holy city on the banks of the Godavari. In the Ramayana, Lord Rama lived here at Panchavati during his exile.

  • Hosts the Kumbh Mela every 12 years
  • Trimbakeshwar, one of the 12 Jyotirlingas
  • Vineyards, grapes and onions grown across the region
